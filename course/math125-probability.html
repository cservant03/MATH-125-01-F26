    Definition 332 Probability.

    🔗
    🔗

    Probability with Equally Likely Outcomes.

    For a finite sample space \(S\) with equally likely outcomes,
    \begin{gather*} P(E)=\dfrac{\text{number of outcomes in }E}{\text{total number of outcomes in }S}. \end{gather*}
    🔗
    \begin{gather*} 0\le P(E)\le 1,\qquad P(\varnothing)=0,\qquad P(S)=1. \end{gather*}
    🔗
    Multiply a probability by 100 to express it as a percent. Keep exact values until the final step, then round as requested.
    🔗

    Complement Rule.

    The complement of \(A\) is denoted by \(A^c\text{.}\)
    \begin{gather*} P(A^c)=1-P(A). \end{gather*}

    Addition Rule.

    \begin{gather*} P(A\text{ or }B)=P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B). \end{gather*}
    🔗
    For mutually exclusive events,
    \begin{gather*} P(A\text{ or }B)=P(A)+P(B). \end{gather*}

    Definition 347 Conditional Probability.

    🔗
    🔗

    Conditional Probability Formula.

    Read \(P(B\mid A)\) as “the probability of \(B\) given \(A\text{.}\)” If \(P(A)>0\text{,}\) then
    \begin{gather*} P(B\mid A)=\dfrac{P(A\cap B)}{P(A)}. \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗

    Multiplication Rule for Independent Events.

    For independent events \(A\) and \(B\text{,}\)
    \begin{gather*} P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\cdot P(B). \end{gather*}
