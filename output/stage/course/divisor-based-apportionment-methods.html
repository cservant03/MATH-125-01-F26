    🔗
    Jefferson’s, Webster’s, and Huntington-Hill methods are divisor-based methods. Each begins with the standard divisor and standard quotas. The quotas are rounded using a rule specific to the method. If the rounded allocations do not use the required number of items, the divisor is adjusted and the quotas are recalculated.
