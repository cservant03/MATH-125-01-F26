    Subsection Majority

    Definition 7. Majority.

    🔗
    🔗
    A choice can receive more first-place votes than every other choice without receiving a majority. To have a majority, the choice must receive more than half of all first-place votes.
    🔗

    Definition 12. Condorcet Winner.

    🔗
    🔗
    In a pairwise comparison, imagine that only two choices remain. Each voter supports whichever of those two choices appears higher on that voter’s preference ballot. A choice does not need to be ranked first on a ballot to receive that voter’s support in a particular pairwise comparison.
    🔗
